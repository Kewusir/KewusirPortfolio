Unity Developer · HCI Researcher · Interaction Designer

A first-person BCI experience where Alpha and Beta activity influenced gameplay in real time through an OpenBCI to Python to Unity pipeline.

  • Built threshold logic, calibration flow, keyboard fallback and player feedback.

Biometric PCVR Horror Experience

Project Manager · Technical Developer · Systems Integrator

A 15 to 20 minute immersive horror system where muscle activity triggered physical interaction in PCVR, combining custom hardware assembly with repeated reliability testing.

  • Built and assembled the EMG sensing hardware used by the experience.
  • Tested false positives, missed detections, reset logic and contraction timing.
  • Integrated sensor input, gameplay events, fallback control and audio logic.

Spatial Audio & Immersion Research

Researcher · Experimental Designer · UX Evaluator

Research into how sound reproduction format changes localisation, spatial perception, presence, immersion and overall user experience in audiovisual and interactive contexts.

  • Designed comparative experiments for VR binaural vs stereo, and stereo vs 5.1.
  • Worked on ethics, participant procedures, testing, interpretation and reporting.
  • Used qualitative and quantitative evidence to inform immersive media evaluation.
