Spatial Audio & Immersion Research

VR, Experimental Design and User Experience Evaluation

This research explored how different sound reproduction formats affect localisation, spatial perception, immersion, presence and overall user experience in audiovisual and interactive contexts.

( contribution )

What I worked on

This project focused on how audio format changes user experience rather than just how it sounds technically. My work covered the research pipeline from question design through testing, interpretation and reporting.

  • Designed comparative experiments for VR binaural vs stereo, and stereo vs 5.1.
  • Worked on research questions, participant materials, ethics preparation and test planning.
  • Collected and interpreted qualitative and quantitative evidence from participant sessions.
  • Used findings to support stronger immersive media evaluation and HCI reasoning.

Research Flow

Question → Experiment → Participants → Analysis

Evaluation Lens

Localisation, spatial perception, presence, immersion and UX interpretation.
