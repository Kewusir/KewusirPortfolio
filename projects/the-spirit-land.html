The Spirit Land

EEG-Controlled First-Person Interactive Experience

The Spirit Land is a first-person BCI experience where Alpha and Beta brain activity are translated into gameplay states in real time. The project sits between Unity development, HCI research and physiological interaction design.

Unity Python OpenBCI EEG HCI

Role

Unity Developer · HCI Researcher · Interaction Designer

Focus

Real-time sensor input, control logic, player feedback and usability.

What It Shows

BCI integration, adaptive interaction design and evidence-based iteration.

( contribution )

What I worked on

My contribution covered both the interactive system and the experience around it. I worked across concept development, Unity implementation, Python integration, control logic, UI feedback and evaluation.

  • Built the gameplay logic that responded to Alpha and Beta activity in real time.
  • Designed calibration, threshold tuning and fallback control to make the system more reliable.
  • Worked on narrative framing, environment feel, visual cues, audio feedback and usability testing.
  • Used participant sessions to review control, frustration, agency and immersion.

Signal Flow

EEG → OpenBCI → Python → Unity

Research Angle

Control stability, usability, player perception and immersive response.
